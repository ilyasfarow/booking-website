Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2025

Bristol Community Events (BCE) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email, encrypted password
  • Transaction Data: Bookings, tickets, payments
  • Usage Data: Device, IP, interaction logs

How We Use Your Data

  • Process bookings and payments
  • Manage discounts and waiting lists
  • Improve system performance
  • Prevent fraud and secure accounts

We never sell your data. Limited sharing only occurs with venues for entry validation.

Terms & Conditions

By using BCE services, you agree to the following terms.

Bookings

Bookings are confirmed only after successful payment. Capacity limits are enforced automatically.

Cancellations

  • 40+ days: No fee
  • 25–39 days: Up to 40% fee
  • < 25 days: 100% fee

Discounts

Students receive a 10% discount (ID required at entry).

Admin Adjustments

Prices may be reduced if event capacity is below 50% within 10 days.

Cookie Policy

We use cookies to improve your experience and ensure the platform functions correctly.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential: Login & booking functionality
  • Performance: Analytics
  • Functional: Preferences

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.
